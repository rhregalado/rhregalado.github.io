excerpt: '<span style="font-family: ''Playfair Display'', serif;"><em>Poetry</em></span> It’s strange how a moving vehicle, of all places, can become the space to also seat down and confront the deeper parts of your thoughts. There is nowhere to rush except toward the destination allowing you to unlearn and relearn some things about yourself along the journey. Today, while going to work, I wrote poetry—or perhaps a litany—about the way a person comes to live within the small, domestic details of my everyday life. What a gift it is to be comfortable with solitude. What a gift it is to step off a two-hour drive feeling, in some small way, reborn. I look forward to the day I finally get to read it to them.'

<div style="text-align: justify;">
<p>I think of you as the Sun,<br>
ever-burning, ever-sufficing,<br>
life-giving, ball of light<br>
you.</p>

<p>I think of you as the Moon,<br>
ever-glowing, ever-pulling,<br>
life-affirming, ball of beauty<br>
you.</p>

<p>I think of you as the color purple,<br>
ever-noble, ever-rare,<br>
life-defining, shade of yearning<br>
you.</p>

<p>I think of you as art,<br>
ever-real, ever-abstract,<br>
life-creating, shade of love<br>
you.</p><br>

<p>(Somewhere in Albay, 08/2026)</p>
